> how to record in obs studio

● Here's how to set up OBS on Windows. It takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Install

  Download it from obsproject.com, choose Windows, and install. On first launch
  the Auto-Configuration Wizard opens. Pick "Optimize for recording" and accept
  the defaults.

  2. Add your screen

  1. At the bottom, in the Sources box, click + and choose Display Capture, then
     OK.
  2. Pick your monitor and click OK. Your desktop should now show in the
     preview.
     - If the preview is black (common on HP laptops with two GPUs), remove that
       source and add Window Capture instead. Or go to Windows Settings →
       Display → Graphics, add obs64.exe, and set it to Power saving. Then
       restart OBS.

  3. Turn on your mic (you have to narrate)

  - Look at the Audio Mixer panel and find Mic/Aux. Talk and check that the
    green bar moves.
  - If Mic/Aux isn't there: go to Settings → Audio → Mic/Auxiliary Audio and
    choose your microphone.
  - If you don't want system sounds recorded, mute Desktop Audio (speaker icon).

  4. Recording settings

  Settings → Output → Recording:
  - Recording Path: a folder with plenty of space, for example F:\Recordings
  - Recording Format: MP4, or MKV to be safe (if OBS crashes, MKV doesn't get
    corrupted; you can convert it with File → Remux Recordings)
  - Settings → Video: Output resolution 1280x720 and FPS 30. That keeps a 2-hour
    file small enough to upload.

  5. Record

  - Click Start Recording (bottom right). A red dot and timer appear at the
    bottom.
  - Do not pause or stop until we're completely done. The recording must be one
    continuous take.
  - At the end, click Stop Recording. The file is saved in your Recording Path.

  Quick test first

  Record 10 seconds, stop, and play the file back. Check that the screen text is
  readable and your voice is audible. Then delete the test file and start the
  real recording.

  After recording

  Upload the file to Google Drive and set sharing to "Anyone with the link can
  view", or to YouTube as Unlisted. Paste that link into README.md.

  When you've started the real recording, reply "recording started" and I'll
  clone the repo.
